# Demo script — M4 (plan core)

Run the web app (`pnpm --filter @plantpal/web dev`, or `VITE_PROVIDERS=mock …` without internet) and open `/welcome` in a fresh browser profile.

1. **Seed onboarding.** "Start my plan" → pick _Balcony_ and _Windowsill or indoors_ → search "Pune" → for the balcony choose "Work it out", face **W**, open sky across: the estimate shows about 4–5 h of direct sun across the year. For the window choose "Bright, little direct sun" → medium pot, tap, 30 min, **Cats** → goals _Grow food_ and _Help pollinators_.
2. **Matches.** About a dozen plants fit, each with "why" chips, timing ("Plant now" or "Plant in September") and a "Check safety" badge where toxicity for cats is not confirmed by a reviewed source. "Updated with local climate" appears once the climate profile arrives. Open "Excluded: see why": _Lantana_ is listed as invasive in India (GRIIS India), _Easter lily_ is toxic to cats (ASPCA), cherry tomatoes need 6 h of direct sun. Save. The sprout grows, and Today shows "Your next three steps".
3. **Providers down.** Block every `*.open-meteo.com` host in devtools and start again: place search says it needs a connection; answer hemisphere, frost and hot-summer questions instead, pick "Part sun · 3–6 h" and save a plan anyway.
4. **Light 0.** Choose "No direct sun" for a courtyard: basil and other sun lovers are excluded with "Needs 6 h of direct sun; this spot gets about 0 h."
5. **Sun Hours Finder.** Plan → Sun Hours Finder: switch facing, add a covered overhang (try 45° on a south-facing London balcony: summer midday sun disappears, winter sun stays), add obstacles by bearing, height and distance, or enter what you saw on clear days. The sun path dome shows solstice and equinox arcs with blocked parts faded; the monthly bars have a table equivalent. Save to the space.
6. **Plan page.** Change goals or household and watch statuses and ranking update; save another plan; older plans say when the space or climate has changed since.

Automated evidence: unit tests covering the catalogue validator, the Sun Hours acceptance fixtures (Pune day length within 5 min, a 60° western wall, Sydney north-facing, a 45° overhang, DST), the eligibility rules with property tests and four regional fixtures, the climate profile and plan storage. Playwright runs W1 under 90 s, a providers-down path, light 0, the Sun Hours Finder and the Plan page with axe, on mobile and desktop.
