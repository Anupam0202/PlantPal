# Demo script: M10 (accounts, sync, sharing, plant-sitter, realtime, push)

Accounts need the Worker with a secret. Run it locally like this:

```sh
pnpm --filter @plantpal/web build
cd apps/api
echo "BETTER_AUTH_SECRET=$(openssl rand -hex 32)" > .dev.vars
pnpm --filter @plantpal/api vapid:keys   # optional: add both lines to .dev.vars for push
pnpm exec wrangler d1 migrations apply plantpal --local
pnpm exec wrangler dev                   # http://localhost:8787 (use "localhost", not 127.0.0.1: passkeys need a domain)
```

1. **Guest first.** Create a garden with a space and a plant, and log care. Nothing leaves the device.
2. **Create an account with a passkey (W9).**
   - Go to Settings › **Account & sync**. Type a name, then **Create account with a passkey**. The browser or phone asks for a fingerprint, face or PIN.
   - There is no password and no email.
   - "Signed in as …".
3. **Back up and sync.**
   - **Back up and sync** uploads the device's gardens through the validated push path.
   - The status goes from "Not synced yet" to "Up to date".
   - Sign in on a second browser, and the garden appears under **Your gardens › Open**.
4. **Invite someone.**
   - **Sharing › Invite someone**: choose Helper, and the link works for 3 days.
   - Copy the link (`/join#…`; shown once, works once) and open it in another browser. Create an account there, then **Accept invitation**.
   - The helper can log care, but changes to spaces are refused and listed under **Changes not sent** with the reason.
5. **Edit at the same time.**
   - Rename the same space on two devices while one is offline, then reconnect.
   - The second device shows **Changed on another device**: **Use mine** or **Keep theirs**.
   - If you both mark the same task done, the second completion gives way to the first.
6. **Plant-sitter link.**
   - **Sharing › Plant-sitter links**: name "Ravi", tick the railing, pick the dates (at most 30 days), then **Create sitter link**.
   - Open `/sit#…` on a phone with no account. It shows only the railing's plants and **Watered / Fed / Checked for pests** buttons.
   - Back on the owner's device, **What they logged** lists "Plant sitter (Ravi)". The care also appears in the journal after sync.
   - **Turn off**: the sitter's page now says the link isn't active.
7. **Live updates.** With two devices open on the same garden, log care on one and the other refreshes within seconds (GardenRoom ping). With the network blocked, the minute-by-minute check covers it.
8. **Notifications.**
   - With VAPID keys set, go to **Notifications › Turn on notifications here**, then **Send a test notification**.
   - On iPhone and iPad, add PlantPal to the Home Screen first.
9. **Remove someone.**
   - The owner removes the helper. The helper's live connection closes.
   - Their unsent changes move to **Changes not sent**, where they can be exported or discarded.
   - Records they added stay with the garden.
