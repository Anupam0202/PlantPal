# Demo script — M7 (health and identification)

Demo mode works for everything except AI (`VITE_PROVIDERS=mock pnpm --filter @plantpal/web dev`). The AI steps need a Gemini key in Settings › AI; the Playwright suite mocks Gemini.

1. **Start a health case.** Garden › open a plant › **Health › New health case** (or Garden › **Health** tab, or the command palette).
   - Add photos in the guided slots: whole plant, affected leaf top and underside, stem base, soil.
   - Tap the signs, for example **Wilting** and **Crisp brown edges**, then set soil **Dry**.
   - **What PlantPal’s rules suggest** updates as you answer: "Too little water · Likely", with evidence for and against, how to tell, safe first steps, and when you'll be reminded to look again.
2. **AI second look (F4).** **Ask AI for a second look** opens the send preview. Photos and answers are needed; weather, care log and space are optional.
   - Switch **Photo** off and the feature is blocked: nothing is sent, and the rules still help.
   - Send it to see the AI draft with likelihood classes only, non-chemical steps and "Ask an expert" with region-keyed services.
3. **Save the case.** The timeline shows the photos, the answers, the rule triage and the AI draft.
   - Follow-up checks are added to Today.
   - Add a follow-up ("Better" plus a note); the case becomes **Watching**.
   - With two or more photos, a before/after slider compares the first and latest.
   - Close the case with an outcome, which stays private.
4. **Photo ID (F5).** Garden › **Add a plant** › add a photo › **Identify from a photo**.
   - Only names GBIF confirms are listed; a count shows what was left out.
   - **Use this name** fills the scientific name and links the catalogue entry when there is one.
   - Typing a name also suggests catalogue matches to link.
5. **Species pages.** Nature › **Plants A–Z** › any plant shows:
   - a container care summary;
   - facts with evidence badges and review state;
   - cited toxicity per group, with unknown groups named;
   - status in your region;
   - the GBIF match, Wikidata common names, and Commons photos with author and licence;
   - sources.

   **I grow this** adds it to your garden.

6. **Draft a missing plant (F3).** Plants A–Z › **Draft a plant that is not listed** › type a scientific name › **Check the name** (GBIF) › **Draft facts with cited sources**.
   - The preview shows that only the name is sent.
   - The draft page lists every fact as **AI draft** with its sources: **Accept** or **Reject** each one.
   - Toxicity can never be accepted from an AI draft.

Automated evidence:

- **Unit tests:**
  - rules triage, including a property test over every answer combination;
  - follow-up dates and advice services;
  - F4, F5 and F3 validators: abstention, percentages, chemical steps, binomial names, citations outside the grounding set, edibility;
  - grounded research request and sources;
  - GBIF match, Commons licence filter, HTML stripping and Wikidata names;
  - health case and species draft commands, legacy cases parsing with new defaults, and the Dexie v5 upgrade.
- **`pnpm eval`:** 160 cases, including 19 F4, 10 F5 and 7 F3 cases (partial JSON, abstention, consent denied, injection in notes). All gates pass.
- **Playwright (both viewports):**
  - W5 health case end to end, including that no GPS data reaches Gemini;
  - the consent-denied path;
  - partial JSON never stored, plus AI abstention after a reload;
  - species page; F3 draft and review; F5 photo ID with GBIF filtering.
