# PlantPal 3.0.0 "Living Field Guide" — release notes (draft)

> Not released. This build is not yet pilot-ready (see `docs/release-gates.md`), and publishing a release, deploying or tagging needs the owner's approval.

PlantPal is rebuilt from a single-file app into an offline-first, evidence-first garden companion for small spaces. Your garden lives on your device; an account is optional.

## New

- **Today:** a care cockpit with "check by" watering, frost and heat guidance from your forecast, holiday catch-up, and a kind weekly look back.
- **Plan:** a 90-second start, plants matched to your light, space, climate and household with reasons, the Sun Hours Finder, a sowing calendar and Climate Fit.
- **Space designer:** plans to scale with checks, sun and shade over the year, growth over time, versions and exports.
- **Health and identification:** guided health cases with rule-based triage, optional AI second looks, photo ID that names only confirmed species, and species pages with cited facts.
- **Nature:** timed pollinator and bird counts where zero counts count, a bloom calendar for pollinators, the Native & Invasive Lens, nature nearby, a green corridor map, and an honest impact ledger.
- **Harvest, seeds and lineage:** harvests by weight or count, a pantry, a seed library with germination tests and family trees.
- **Garden Copilot:** ask about your own garden; it looks things up on your device and suggests changes you confirm. Uses your own Gemini key, straight from your device to Google.
- **Field stamps and lessons:** stamps earned from your records with no streaks; six short field lessons.
- **Garden Story and labels:** your year as an image card or PDF, and printable plant labels with QR codes.
- **Optional accounts:** passkeys only, sync across devices, shared gardens with roles, plant-sitter links, live updates and push notifications.
- **Community (off by default):** a moderated feed, events and a Seed & Cutting Exchange with mutual-consent contacts. The operator switches it on only after adopting a policy and naming moderators.

## Moving from v3

Open the new app in the same browser: it finds your old data, shows what it will import, saves a backup file first and keeps your old XP and badges read-only. Your old Gemini key is only moved into the encrypted vault if you agree.

## Privacy and security

No trackers or analytics. Locations are rounded before they leave your device. A strict Content Security Policy with Trusted Types; the service worker and web workers now run under it (a fix in this release). See `docs/privacy.md`.

## Known limitations

- Slow first load on slower phones (about 4–6 s on a throttled mid-range profile); a pre-rendered shell is planned.
- No screen-reader review by a person yet.
- Photos stay on the device that took them (no photo sync yet).
- Garden Walk (voice) and Pl@ntNet are waiting on a decision about how keys are sent.
- The app does not diagnose plant problems with certainty, certify safety or edibility, guarantee ecological outcomes, provide unlimited free capacity or guarantee that reminders are delivered.
