## What the community is for

Growers near each other sharing seeds, cuttings, seedlings, spare produce and tools for free, asking and answering questions, and organising local events such as seed swaps and work days.

## Who can take part

- People with a PlantPal account (passkey) and a community profile.
- New accounts can post after a waiting period (24 hours by default).
- Everyone can read, report and block. Posting is limited to 10 items an hour.

## What you share, and who sees it

- Your display name, your optional bio and what you post are visible to people in your area.
- Your area is a hexagon of about 5 km², worked out on your device. Your address and exact location are never sent.
- The map shows an area only when at least five people are active there, and only as a count.
- Contact details are shown only to the other person in a swap, and only after you have both chosen to share them.
- There are no private messages.

## Not allowed

1. **Spam or advertising**, including selling. The exchange is for giving.
2. **Harassment or hate**: threats, slurs, targeting a person or group, sharing someone's private information.
3. **Invasive or unsafe plants**: species listed as invasive where you live (PlantPal refuses known ones automatically), plants or seeds whose sharing is restricted by law, and unlabelled or misleading plant material.
4. **Illegal content or activity**, including controlled plants.
5. **Harmful misinformation**, such as claims that a toxic plant is safe to eat or that a plant cures a disease.
6. **Fake activity**: invented events, listings or accounts.

PlantPal records are not official phytosanitary documents or plant passports. Check local rules before sending or carrying plant material across borders.

## How moderation works

- **Reports.** Anyone can report a post, event, listing or question and choose a reason. Three independent reports hide it until a moderator reviews it.
- **Decisions.** Moderators can keep, hide or remove content, warn the author, pause posting for a set number of days, or ban an account. Every decision records a reason in an audit log that admins can read.
- **Telling you.** You see each decision about your content or account on the community page, with the reason.
- **Appeals.** You can appeal each decision once. A different moderator should review it where possible. Accepted appeals reverse the decision.
- **Blocks.** Blocking someone hides their content from you and yours from them, and stops swaps between you.
- **Target response time:** _owner to set (for example, reports reviewed within 72 hours)_.

## Safety at handoffs

- Meet in a public place in daylight, or leave items at a public pickup point.
- Never put your home address in a listing. The pickup field asks for a public place or general area.
- You can release or cancel a reservation at any time before the handoff.

## Data and deletion

> **Before launch (not built yet):** self-service account deletion and a scheduled purge of removed content. Both are launch prerequisites in the scope ledger; do not publish the promises below until they exist.

- Community content is kept while your account exists.
- Removed content stays hidden from everyone and is kept for _owner to set, for example 90 days_ so appeals and the audit log can refer to it, then deleted.
- Deleting your account will delete your profile, posts, events, listings, questions, reservations and blocks. Moderation audit entries keep only the action and the reason.

## Changes

The operator will announce changes to this policy on the community page at least 14 days before they take effect, except for urgent safety changes.
