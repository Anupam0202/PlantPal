# Code of Conduct

PlantPal is a community for gardeners, naturalists and developers. We want it to be welcoming, kind and useful.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners.
- Share knowledge generously and cite sources when you make factual claims.
- Disagree about ideas, never attack people.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexualised content, doxxing (including sharing someone's precise location), spam or scams.
- Never promote the sale or exchange of invasive or illegal plants.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation for behaviour that breaks these standards. Report problems privately to the maintainer via the contact listed in `SECURITY.md` or a private GitHub security advisory. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
